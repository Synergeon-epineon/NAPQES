Data Sources & Methodology
  1. IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report 2026 — OT/ICS average breach cost $4.5M; shadow AI governance gap $670K; sector multipliers.
  2. European Commission NIS2 Directive (EU 2022/2555) — maximum fine 2% annual global revenue or €10M whichever higher.
  3. DORA Regulation (EU 2022/2554) — operational resilience requirements for financial entities from Jan 2025.
  4. NSA CNSA 2.0 (Commercial National Security Algorithm Suite) — mandatory PQC transition timeline to 2030.
  5. AI Act (EU 2024/1689) — General Purpose AI compliance obligations; fines up to 3% turnover for non-systemic violations.
  6. Verizon DBIR 2026 — VPN infrastructure breach probability 8% annual; average cost $1.5M.
  7. MITRE ATT&CK for ICS — lateral movement probability post-initial access used for breach multiplier estimates.
  8. NPV computed at 8% annual discount rate; break-even in months = ceil(Y0 cost / monthly net saving).
  9. This simulator provides indicative analysis only. Actual ROI depends on implementation quality, threat environment, and organisational readiness.
